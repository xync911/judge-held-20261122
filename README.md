A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it, entered in
[make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel held" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600),
$50 shared by the top 20.

The text shows a quiet scene instead of instructing anyone to relax: a
porch light burning for no one, moths circling slowly, the smell of rain
that fell hours ago, an empty chair in the warm dark — and nothing asked
of the reader. 226 characters.

`main.py` copies `entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt` — no network, no
clock, so two runs write identical bytes.
